How It Works

Three Simple Steps

  1. Start with a Full Detail or Deep Interior Clean. We get your vehicle to a clean starting point. Already detailed with us? You can join right away.
  2. Pick your schedule. Every 2 weeks, every month or every 2 months.
  3. We come to you, on schedule. You pay after each visit. No card on file and no monthly charge.

Every club visit includes

  • Interior vacuum and wipe-down
  • Cupholders and interior glass
  • Hand wash, wheels and tires
  • Tire dressing and exterior glass
  • Spray wax

Not included: shampooing, extraction or stain treatment. Need a deeper clean? Book a Full Detail any time.

Missed a spot? Tell us within 24 hours and we come back and clean it, free.

Good to Know

Tuff Club Questions

Who can join?

Anyone in our service area who has had a Full Detail or Deep Interior Clean with us: North Little Rock, Little Rock, Sherwood, Maumelle, Jacksonville, Conway, Cabot, Bryant and Benton. New customers can sign up when they book their first detail; club prices start on the next visit.

How do I pay?

After each visit, once the work is done. There's no card on file and no monthly charge.

Is there a contract?

Tuff Club needs at least 3 visits. If you stop before your third visit, we charge the difference between the club price and the regular price on the visits you've had. After that, cancel anytime with a text.

Can I skip or move a visit?

Yes. Text us at least 24 hours ahead and we'll move it. If weather gets in the way, we reschedule with you.

What if my vehicle gets extra dirty between visits?

Pet hair and heavy sand start at $55 each, and heavy dirt, mud or buildup is $50 per extra 30 minutes, the same as any booking. We always go over any change with you before we start.

Ready to stop scheduling detail days?

Book your first detail and pick a schedule. We'll set up your club visits when we confirm.
