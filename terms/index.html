Service terms

These terms apply to quotes, appointments and gift cards from Tuff Bros Mobile Detailing.

Quotes and appointments

A quote request or online booking request is not a confirmed appointment, and the day and time you choose online are a preference. Tuff Bros reviews the vehicle details, condition, photos and service location, then confirms the price and time directly with you by phone or text.

Deposits and payment

Once Tuff Bros confirms your appointment time, we text you a link to pay a $25 deposit to hold it. The deposit is applied to your total, and the remaining balance is due after the service is complete.

Cancelling and rescheduling

If you cancel or reschedule with at least 24 hours' notice, your deposit is refunded or moved to your new appointment. If you cancel or reschedule with less than 24 hours' notice, or the vehicle is not available at the appointment, the deposit is kept. If Tuff Bros reschedules because of weather, your deposit moves to the new appointment.

Pricing and condition

Any price shown online is a starting estimate, not a final quote. Full-size SUVs and minivans are priced $50 above the SUV/truck price; vans and lifted or dually trucks are quoted individually. Final pricing depends on vehicle size, condition, access, travel, and approved add-ons. Prices assume a vehicle in normal, everyday condition; we look it over when we arrive, and heavy dirt, mud or buildup is $50 per extra 30 minutes. We go over any change with you before we start. Heavy soil, pet hair, sand, stains, odors, mold, biohazards, oversized vehicles, and custom services require review and may be routed to a custom quote. Biohazard cleanup is not offered.

Vehicle access and service limitations

Please provide accurate vehicle details and clear photos, ensure safe access to the vehicle, and remove personal belongings where practical. Tuff Bros does not guarantee permanent stain, odor, pet-hair, sand, scratch, paint-defect, or pre-existing-damage removal. If a concern relates to work included in the purchased service, contact Tuff Bros within 24 hours so it can be reviewed.

Our missed-spot guarantee

If we missed something included in the service you booked, tell us within 24 hours of the appointment and we'll come back and clean it at no charge. This covers areas included in your service; it does not cover the limitations listed above.

Maintenance services

Basic Wash & Wax, Quick Interior Clean and Basic In & Out are maintenance visits for returning customers whose vehicle has had a full or deep detail with Tuff Bros. New customers start with a Full Detail, Deep Interior Clean or Wash, Clay & Seal.

Tuff Club

Tuff Club is a Basic In & Out on a schedule (every 2 weeks, every month or every 2 months) at the club price shown on the Tuff Club page, for customers who have had a Full Detail or Deep Interior Clean with Tuff Bros. You pay after each visit; no card is kept on file. There is no minimum number of visits, and you can cancel anytime. The club price applies while each visit happens within 2 weeks of its scheduled date (1 week for every-2-weeks members). If a visit is skipped or happens later than that, that visit is charged at the regular price, and the club price resumes with the next visit on schedule. To move a visit, give at least 24 hours' notice. If Tuff Bros reschedules for weather, your club price does not change. If a vehicle has been let go, Tuff Bros may recommend a Full Detail first. Extras and condition charges apply as on any booking.

Gift cards

Gift cards are sold through Stripe and are good for 5 years from the purchase date shown on the certificate, with no fees. They can be used toward any Tuff Bros service in our service area: North Little Rock, Little Rock, Sherwood, Maumelle, Jacksonville, Conway, Cabot, Bryant and Benton. Quick maintenance services are for returning customers, so a gift card first covers a full or deep detail; any remaining balance stays on the card for later visits. If a service costs more than the card, the difference is paid at the appointment. Gift cards are not redeemable for cash except where required by law.

Questions
