Faded Trim Makes a Vehicle Look Older

Sun, heat, and weather can turn black bumpers, mirror housings, cowl panels, running boards, and other exterior plastics gray or chalky. A detail can clean the surface, but restoration is for trim that needs its color and consistency brought back.

What We Restore

  • Front and rear bumpers — faded, gray, or uneven black plastic
  • Grilles and cowl panels — exterior plastic that has lost its rich finish
  • Mirror housings and door trim — weathered pieces that stand out against clean paint
  • Step trim and lower cladding — the durable plastic around trucks and SUVs

We thoroughly clean and prepare the plastic before applying the appropriate trim-restoration treatment. Results and durability vary with the trim’s age, condition, texture, and sun exposure, so photos help us give you an accurate quote.

Good to Know

Trim Restoration Questions

Can faded plastic trim really be restored?

Often, yes. We can significantly improve faded exterior plastic when the trim is structurally sound. Deep damage, cracking, or severely weathered material may limit the result, and we will set expectations before starting.

How long does trim restoration last?

Durability depends on the vehicle’s storage, sun exposure, washing habits, and the condition of the plastic. We will recommend aftercare that helps the finish last.

How much does trim restoration cost?

Pricing depends on how much trim needs attention and its condition. Text us clear photos for an accurate quote before booking.
