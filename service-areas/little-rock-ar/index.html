Frequently Asked Questions

Do you really come to Little Rock?

Yes — Little Rock is part of our regular service area. We bring our own water, power, and equipment to your home, office, or job site.

Can you detail at my office downtown?

Usually, yes — we detail in office parking lots and decks whenever the property allows it and there's room to set up. Ask your building first, then send us the details.

How do I get the fastest quote in Little Rock?

Text photos of your vehicle to (501) 500-4306 with your city and what you're looking for. Photos let us quote accurately the first time.

How much does car detailing cost in Little Rock?

First visits start at $180 for a deep interior clean and run up to $300 for a full detail on a car ($350 for SUVs and trucks). Maintenance visits for returning customers start at $75. Ceramic coating starts at $749. Text photos to (501) 500-4306 for an exact quote.

Do you offer ceramic coating in West Little Rock?

Yes. For ceramic coating and paint correction, a garage or covered location is strongly recommended. If you don’t have one, ask us about drop-off before booking.

What if it rains?

Arkansas weather happens. If conditions won’t allow quality work, we’ll reach out and reschedule at no charge.
