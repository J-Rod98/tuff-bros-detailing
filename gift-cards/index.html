Good to Know

Gift Card Questions

Do gift cards expire?

Each card is good for 5 years from the purchase date, and the expiration date is printed on the certificate. There are no fees.

What if the detail costs more or less than the card?

If it costs more, they pay the difference at the appointment. If it costs less, the remaining balance stays on the card for next time.

Can the card be used for a quick wash or interior clean?

Our quick maintenance services are for returning customers. A gift card covers their first full or deep detail, and any remaining balance can go toward maintenance visits after that.

Where can the card be used?

Anywhere we serve: North Little Rock, Little Rock, Sherwood, Maumelle, Jacksonville, Conway, Cabot, Bryant and Benton. If they're nearby but not listed, just ask.

I lost the certificate. Can you look it up?

Yes. Text us the name and email used at checkout and we'll find the gift code.

Can I buy for a business or a group?

Yes. For several cards or employee gifts, text or call (501) 500-4306 and we'll set it up.
